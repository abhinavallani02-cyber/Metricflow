## Findings and interpretation

1. **Country concentration:** United Kingdom accounts for £9,001,744.09 (84.59%) of gross valid sales, across 18,019 invoices. This describes a geographically concentrated observed revenue mix; it does not establish market size or untapped demand.
2. **Seasonal pattern:** 2011-11 is the largest complete observed calendar month at £1,503,866.78 and 2,769 orders. A single annual cycle cannot establish repeatable seasonality or causation; December 2011 is incomplete and is not a comparable full month.
3. **Product concentration:** stock code DOT (DOTCOM POSTAGE) leads revenue at £206,248.77. Stock code 23843 leads units at 80,995. Revenue and unit rankings answer different questions; cost and margin data are absent.

**Audit evidence behind the unit leader:**

| Invoice | Quantity | Source timestamp | Treatment |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 581483 | 80,995 | 2011-12-09 09:15:00 | valid_sale |
| C581484 | -80,995 | 2011-12-09 09:27:00 | cancellation |

An equal opposite-quantity cancellation appears shortly after the large positive sale. The gross-sales policy retains the positive line and excludes the cancellation; this ranking must not be interpreted as retained demand. No general return-to-sale matching is claimed.

4. **Observed repeat purchasing:** 2,845 of 4,338 identifiable customers placed at least two distinct orders (65.58%). This is repeat purchasing within the observed window, not retention, churn, or lifetime behavior.

## Limitations and next steps

- Cancellations/returns are excluded instead of netted. Gross sales can overstate retained customer spend. Pairing returns to original invoices requires separate work.
- Exact duplicates are removed after their first occurrence; legitimate repeated lines might be indistinguishable.
- Positive non-merchandise stock codes (postage, fees) remain in sales and product rankings. This prevents undocumented exclusions but limits interpretation as merchandise-only sales.
- Missing customer IDs bias customer measures; geographic results use invoice country, not verified residence.
- No fulfillment status, costs, traffic, profit, conversion, customer acquisition cost, or causal evidence is available.
- Compare the preserved workbook checksum against an official UCI download when network access permits. Investigate product description changes and return matching before extending the metrics.
- Validation: SQL totals reconciled across monthly/country aggregations and independently reconstructed Pandas revenue, distinct invoices, and identifiable customers.
